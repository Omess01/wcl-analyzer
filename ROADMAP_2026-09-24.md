# Roadmap — 2026-09-24

> What to build next, in phases, after `REVIEW_2026-09-24.md`. Each item: effort (S < 1 h, M = an evening, L = several sessions), what it
> needs from WCL / Raider.IO (**none** = data already collected), and why a raid lead or raider would care. Items marked ★ are the
> best value per hour in their phase. Every phase should get its own `docs/superpowers/plans/<date>-<name>.md` before code is written,
> and every change a tagged entry in `CHANGES_<date>.md`.
>
> Principles carried over: the dashboard is a **public artefact** (anonymous Home by default, under 10 MB); **one renderer** per
> section (browser for boss tabs); **tests stay offline**; **console output is the audit trail**; **no hex colours outside
> `tokens.css`** once the redesign lands.
